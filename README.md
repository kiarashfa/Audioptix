# Music Player

**Audioptix** (from *audio* + *optics*) is a clean, browser-based music player for your own local files.

Just drag and drop your tracks to build a playlist. It reads embedded cover art and tags, drives a live audio visualizer, and ships with a handful of swappable themes. No uploads, no accounts, no streaming — everything stays on your device.

Press play and enjoy 🎵.
